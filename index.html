ABOUT COMPANY

The Ultimate Button Source for Garment Makers

Founded in 1998, Z&J Button Industries has grown into one of South Asia’s most trusted high-precision garment fastener manufacturers. Operating from our state-of-the-art facility in Karachi, we combine Italian CNC turning technology, automated laser engraving, and eco-conscious dyeing systems to supply export-grade buttons to premier fashion houses and vertically integrated textile mills worldwide.

Whether producing high-luster chalk polyester shirt buttons, antique-finished brass denim shanks, or sustainable corozo and buffalo horn trims, every batch undergoes rigorous spectrophotometric color matching and mechanical pull-strength testing.
